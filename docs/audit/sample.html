5. Audit verdict and remediation

Observed: the original Delivery has one confirmed chunk and one unresolved unknown after restart.

Prevented: no automatic model replay and no second attempt against original Delivery 6.

Explicit recovery: /result 22 creates Delivery 7 with two unique message ids.

Unresolved: successful explicit recovery does not prove the original unknown arrived.

Recommendation: keep Run and Delivery identities separate; require explicit owner action or channel-proven reconciliation before repeating an external effect.
